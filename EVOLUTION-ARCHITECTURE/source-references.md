# Primary references and boundaries

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework): accountable risk management and evaluation. Voluntary framework; no certification asserted.
- [Large Language Models as Optimizers / OPRO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.03409): model-generated candidate optimization on studied tasks. Not proof of universal improvement or unattended production safety.
- [Just Ask for Calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14975): confidence elicitation research. This design independently measures calibration against resolved outcomes.
- [W3C PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/): entity/activity/agent and derivation vocabulary for provenance.

References inform design choices. Platform-specific protocols, score definitions and release policy are proposed engineering decisions, not claims that those sources validate OmniDev Swarm. Retrieved 2026-10-09.
